0. Meaning of Communication :-  

- Sending and receiving information between 2 or more people.  
- The person sending the message is referred to as the sender, while the person receiving the information is called the receiver.  
- Information includes facts, ideas, concepts, opinions, beliefs, attitudes, instructions and emotions.
