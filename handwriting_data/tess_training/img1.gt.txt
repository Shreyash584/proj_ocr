Five Sense of Communication
1 - Taste
2 - Touch
3 - Hear
4 - Sight
5 - Smell

➡ Non-Verbal Communication
   a) By expression on our face.
   b) By body movements

➡ Advantage of oral Communication
1 - It is spontaneous and natural
2 - Easy for others to understand
3 - Choice of words suits the listener
4 - Supported by non-verbal comm
5 - Communicator always physically Avail
6 - Develop close relation between speaker & listener

➡ Disadvantage of oral Communication
1 - Words are temporary
2 - What is heard is often forgotten
